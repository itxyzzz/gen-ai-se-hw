# Benchmark Scoring Rubric

This rubric is applied by review to preserved text-pipeline artifacts. It is not
an executable benchmark layer.

- Correctness: 40 points for passing changed-app tests.
- Security remediation: 25 points for no HIGH-or-worse findings.
- Test quality: 15 points for generated tests.
- Maintainability and minimality: 10 points for completed bounded changes.
- Reproducibility and autonomy: 10 points for zero manual interventions.
